Argho Ghosh Project

2nd Semester

CSE-111

Object-Oriented Programming (OOP)


Introduction to basic computer science concepts, problem-solving techniques, and programming fundamentals.
Students will learn to write simple programs and understand core computational logic.

CSE-103

Welcome to CSE-103 : Discrete Mathematics!

Hands-on programming practice to reinforce CSE-111 concepts.
Lab exercises focus on writing, testing, and debugging programs using basic programming constructs and algorithms.

CSE-112

Object-Oriented Programming (OOP)-LAB


Covers essential discrete math topics including sets, logic, functions, relations, combinatorics, and graphs.
Builds analytical skills for algorithms, programming, and computing theory.

EEE-101

Electrical Circuits and Electronics


Introduction to basic electrical circuits and electronics concepts.
Students will learn fundamental principles of electrical engineering and circuit analysis.

EEE-102

Electrical and Electronic Measurements


Introduction to electrical and electronic measurement techniques.
Students will learn to use various measuring instruments and interpret measurement data.

MAT-111

Vector Calculus


Introduction to vectors, their representation in
2D and 3D space. Covers vector addition,
subtraction, scalar and vector products, and applications in physics and computer science.
